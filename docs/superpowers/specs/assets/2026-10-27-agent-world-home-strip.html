What should the Home strip look like?

Three directions for the short, wide live view at the top of Home. Each one shows the same moment: a chat and an image are running, a model is loaded, the printer is at 62%, and two Claude Code sessions are working. Tap the strip and the full Agent World opens.

A

Main Street (side view)

A building per kind of work and per house thing, in a row. A person at the door means something is running there, and lit windows mean recent activity. A car drives off when a run finishes. Side view suits a wide, short strip and stays readable on a phone (it scrolls sideways). It's a new art style, though, and different from the top-down full city.

B

A window onto today's city (top-down)

The same top-down city you already have, cropped to a strip. The camera frames whatever is busiest. It reuses the existing renderer and art, and the strip and full page look identical. The catch: a top-down map cropped this short shows only a few buildings, and labels get small on a phone.
