Mission "Audit Inbox"using the model now
  • Readable: the art shows that something is happening (the furnace pours, crates ride the conveyor). Crisp UI chips say what: the name, the state, the count. Text is never painted into the picture, so it's sharp at any size and works with screen readers.
  • Depth: a hazy far city behind, a side wall and a corrugated roof, a sidewalk with a curb and the road in front, and a bush in the foreground. On the full page these layers drift at different speeds as the camera moves (parallax).
  • Materials and light: brick, steel, glass with reflections, light rays in the hall, the furnace glow spilling onto the floor, soft shadows under everything.
  • Inside each building there's a scene of the actual work. The queue is crates on a belt, the model is the metal being poured, the worker at the door holds a clipboard.
  • Honest cost: this is about what hand-written vector art can reach. For more than this (painterly texture, true realism), I'd move to illustrated image layers made outside the code, with the motion and light still driven by code on top.
